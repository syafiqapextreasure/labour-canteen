These terms are recommendations inferred from the business offering and the language used on competitor sites. Search demand and difficulty have not been measured. Use phrases naturally in page titles, introductory copy, descriptions and relevant image alt text. Do not fill every heading with locations. “Canteen operator” should be used only where on-site operation is actually offered. “Meal allowance” can mean payroll/tax benefits; qualify it as a company-supported meal programme so the service is not confused with tax or HR advisory content.

Most useful differentiation:
- Clear relationship between company management and workers.
- A customisable weekly plan across breakfast, lunch and tea-time.
- Multinational tastes and menu variety rather than a generic catering buffet.
- Worker pays their share at mealtime; employer settles the agreed contribution later.
- Shah Alam central kitchen, up to 1,000 packs daily, Selangor service coverage.
- Real catering experience and truthful individual training summaries.
Do not copy competitors' scale, certification, location coverage or delivery promises.

Who leads the company?
“AZLINHAN AKBAR KHAN is Managing Director. MUHAMMAD ALIYAS NAINA MOHAMED is Director.” Follow with their supplied qualifications.

Google's current guidance says conventional SEO remains relevant to generative search. Helpful first-party information, clear structure and crawlability matter; special AI markup or llms.txt is not required for Google. This supports preserving authentic business facts, accessible text and useful images rather than publishing many near-identical keyword pages. Eligibility does not guarantee inclusion or citations.
Source: Google's Guide to Optimizing for Generative AI Features
https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide

Google's current changelog says FAQ rich results stopped appearing from May 7, 2026, with documentation removed in June. Do not sell FAQPage markup as a way to obtain FAQ rich snippets. Keep FAQs because the answers are useful.
Source: Google Search documentation updates, May/June 2026
https://developers.google.com/search/updates

Bing's guidelines similarly emphasise accurate content and matching structured data, and warn against keyword stuffing, low-value scaled generation and attempts to manipulate AI answers. Bing's AI Performance reporting can later show citation trends and grounding queries; results cannot be assessed before the site is live and indexed.

STRUCTURED DATA IMPLEMENTATION
Use JSON-LD with one stable business entity, for example productionOrigin + "/#business".
Suggested type: FoodEstablishment, a LocalBusiness/Organization subtype describing a food-related business. Do not call it a walk-in Restaurant unless that service is actually offered. A Service node can describe workforce meal catering and reference the business via provider. A Menu node can reference the rendered menu catalogue. These are semantic suggestions, not guaranteed rich-result features.

https://schema.org/Service
https://schema.org/Menu

  streetAddress: Jalan Lontar Peluru 13/39, Seksyen 13

  addressCountry: MY

Omit unprovided phone/email, geocoordinates, openingHours, priceRange, reviews, aggregateRating, company registration numbers and certification fields. Do not add fake Offer prices when pricing is by quotation. Do not put private planner data or client enquiries in public schema.

Put BreadcrumbList on subpages, representing Home → page. Use at least two ListItems. Keep markup consistent with visible page context. Add Organization-compatible logo and URL information to help entity disambiguation. Validate supported markup using Google's Rich Results Test and broader vocabulary using Schema.org Validator.

TECHNICAL CHECKLIST
- Stable clean paths with no .html links; permanent redirects from former .html variants if they ever existed.
- HTTP 200 for valid public pages, real 404 status for unknown routes; no universal 200 fallback for nonexistent content.
- Unique title/description, one primary H1, logical headings, descriptive link labels.
- Crawlable server-rendered/static HTML for the business story, address, FAQs and menu text.
- Self-referencing canonical URLs based on the real production hostname; sitemap with the same canonical URLs.
- robots.txt references the sitemap; no accidental noindex or robots blocks on production.
- Noindex dev/preview copies if accessible publicly; avoid publishing duplicate site origins.
- Descriptive image filenames and natural alt text; decorative shapes/icons use empty alt or aria-hidden.
- Responsive images with width/height, efficient image formats and sensible loading; eager/high priority for the true hero, lazy below the fold.
- The public site must work and retain essential content without animations. Honour reduced-motion preferences.
- EN/BM selector must lead to real translated content. Do not add hreflang for untranslated or placeholder pages. If only EN is implemented, remove or label the inactive BM control rather than pretending it is complete.
- Do not render competitor names or this research as promotional claims on customer-facing pages.
- No doorway pages for every town or industrial estate without genuine local service detail.

AFTER DEPLOYMENT
Confirm the final canonical hostname, verify ownership in Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools, submit sitemap, inspect representative URLs and review indexing. Use actual enquiry conversions and search queries to refine language.
